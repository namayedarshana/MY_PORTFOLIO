MySQL SQL Database
View GitHub

Project Overview

This project is designed to manage and streamline the operations of a fitness center. It focuses on maintaining clear records of members, trainers, workout sessions, membership plans, payments, and feedback.


The project demonstrates how a relational database can be used to organize structured information, manage records and retrieve useful information through SQL queries.

Objective

  • Ensures organized data management for the gym.
  • Helps in tracking performance of trainers and sessions.
  • Provides financial transparency through payment records.
  • Improves member satisfaction with structured feedback.

Dataset

The project is built on six main tables: Member_Details , Trainers_Details, Workout_Sessions, Membership_Plans Payment, and Feedback

SQL

Results & Insights

  • Identify which membership plan has the highest number of enrolled members and revenue generated. This helps the gym understand its most successful plan.
  • Analyze members whose payment status is “Not Paid” or “Partially Paid.” This helps the gym identify pending payments and improve cash-flow management.
  • Compare trainers based on number of members trained and average customer rating. This can help management identify high-performing trainers and areas needing improvement.
  • Identify members who have been assigned trainers but have not provided feedback. The gym can use this information to encourage members to give feedback and improve service quality.
  • Identify trainers receiving ratings below 3/5. Management can investigate the reasons for dissatisfaction and provide additional training or support where required.
  • Analyze VIP members who have assigned trainers, including their workout timings and session end dates. This helps monitor whether premium members are receiving the expected level of personal training service
